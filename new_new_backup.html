Introduction

Prof. Xiangyang Ji  季向阳

Department of Automation, Tsinghua University

Room 825, Main Building, Beijing 100084

xyji@tsinghua.edu.cn  ·  Faculty Homepage

The Intelligent Decision-Making Team (THU-IDM) at Tsinghua University is directed by Professor Xiangyang Ji. Our research is centered on advancing decision-making intelligence, with a particular emphasis on generative modeling, reinforcement learning, and foundation models. The team comprises members with diverse interdisciplinary expertise spanning automation, mathematics, physics, and computer science, and maintains strong collaborative ties with international scholars.
